# Kisan Mitra (🌾) — Phased Implementation Walkthrough

All 5 phases of the **Kisan Mitra** smart agricultural platform have been completed according to `BUILD_PLAN.md`.

---

## 🌟 What Was Built

### Phase 1 — Core Multilingual UI & Rich Static/SQLite Data
- **Multilingual Support (i18n)**: Fully populated localization dictionaries for **English (`en`)**, **తెలుగు / Telugu (`te`)**, and **हिंदी / Hindi (`hi`)** across all components, forms, and results.
- **Persistent Language Selection**: User language preference is automatically saved to `localStorage` and remembered on subsequent visits.
- **Expanded SQLite Knowledge Base**: 16 major Indian crops with detailed symptoms, causes, organic & biological remedies, chemical pesticides, cultural precautions, NPK schedules, and soil requirements.
- **Smart Unified Search**: Live debounced search across Crops, Diseases, Pests, and Outbreak Alerts with categorized dropdown suggestions.
- **Agricultural Disclaimer**: Reusable disclaimer component embedded on all diagnostic, advisory, and recommendation pages.

### Phase 2 — Real Image Analysis & Camera Capture
- **`ImageUpload` Component**:
  - Drag-and-drop file upload with client-side canvas compression.
  - **Live Camera Snapshot Modal**: Captures video stream from device webcam or back/front camera via `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with viewfinder guidelines.
  - **Quick Sample Presets**: 4 instant test samples (Tomato Early Blight, Rice Leaf Blast, Cotton Bollworm, Chilli Leaf Curl) for one-click testing.
- **`POST /api/detect` Diagnostic Engine**:
  - Delivers confidence scores, disease severity rating, organic remedies, chemical treatments, precautions, and growth stage guidelines.

### Phase 3 — Voice Assistant & Multilingual Depth
- **Web Speech API Integration**:
  - Native in-browser `SpeechRecognition` in Telugu (`te-IN`), Hindi (`hi-IN`), and English (`en-IN`).
  - Interactive pulsing microphone animation.
  - Contextual agricultural query intent answering with voice synthesis (`SpeechSynthesisUtterance`).
- **"🔊 Listen in Audio"**: TTS audio player on diagnostic result cards with audio playback controls.

### Phase 4 — Weather API, Seasonal Alerts & Soil Scanner
- **Agricultural Weather Dashboard (`/weather`)**:
  - Live forecast via Open-Meteo with regional selection across major Indian agricultural zones (Hyderabad, Warangal, Guntur, Vijayawada, Kurnool, Bengaluru, Pune, Delhi, Lucknow, Patna).
  - Calculates **Pesticide Spraying Suitability Index**, **Irrigation Planning Advisory**, and **Fungal Disease Risk Alert**.
- **Disease & Pest Outbreak Alerts (`/alerts`)**:
  - Real-time weather-triggered alerts with High/Moderate severity filters and urgent action steps.
- **Crop Cultivation Guide (`/advisory`)**:
  - Stage-by-stage growth timeline (Sowing, Vegetative, Flowering, Harvesting), irrigation intervals, and nutrient plans for all 16 target crops.
- **Smart NPK Fertilizer Calculator (`/fertilizer`)**:
  - Computes pure Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium requirements and translates them into **50kg commercial bags (Urea, DAP, MOP Potash)** based on farm acreage, along with organic alternatives (FYM, Vermicompost, Neem Cake, Jeevamrutham).
- **Soil Health & Texture Scanner (`/soil`)**:
  - Analyzes soil color & texture to estimate pH range, organic carbon, water retention, and recommended crop suitability.
- **Agricultural Q&A Hub (`/ask`)**:
  - Categorized FAQ engine covering pest control, fertilization, soil preparation, and government schemes.

### Phase 5 — Polish, Aesthetics & Responsiveness
- Lush emerald and earthy gold visual design with custom Google Fonts (`Outfit` & `Plus Jakarta Sans`).
- Mobile-responsive navigation drawer with smooth hamburger menu for screens below 640px.
- High-contrast badges, smooth micro-interactions, and fast Vite production bundle build (`353 kB`).
